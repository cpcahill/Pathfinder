What makes a city appealing to me (use these as the "fit" test):
- Good weather and a generally pleasant climate
- Plenty to do: outdoor activities, food, music, sports, nightlife, things to explore
- A genuinely nice place to live, attractive surroundings or scenery
- Affordability relative to the salary offered: a lower salary in an affordable
  city can beat a higher salary in an expensive one. Weigh cost of living against
  pay rather than chasing the biggest number.
- A real job market for analysts, so there is room to grow and switch roles later

Work arrangement:
